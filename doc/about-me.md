## Snapshot

I am twenty, female, and just beginning life as a student. I am optimistic by nature, full of sympathy, and genuinely open to people. Social rooms do not scare me — they wake me up. I like first conversations, second chances, and the feeling that a stranger might become a friend.

## How I come across

People usually meet the smile first. I am warm, easy to talk to, and I mean it when I ask how you are. I listen more than I perform. I remember small details: the exam you were nervous about, the song you mentioned, the way you take your coffee. I am not trying to be impressive. I am trying to be kind, present, and real.

I have a lot of sympathy. If someone is having a quiet, difficult day, I notice. I would rather sit with them than fill the silence with empty advice. Empathy is not a costume for me; it is how I move through a room.

I am optimistic without pretending that everything is easy. I look for the good in people and in ordinary days — a laugh after class, a walk in decent weather, a conversation that goes longer than planned. Hope, for me, is a practical habit: it leaves space for other people to be their best selves too.

## How I am with people

I am open. I like meeting new classmates, saying hello first, holding the door, and making the extra chair feel like it was always meant to be there. Socializing is not a strategy; it is one of the ways I feel most alive. Groups, cafés, shared kitchens, late talks on the stairs — I am at home in those places.

I am curious about other people’s stories. I ask questions. I do not rush to judge. I would rather understand someone than win a point. Loyalty matters to me: once I care about you, I show up.

I laugh easily. I am the person who will celebrate your small wins with you, because small wins are how a life is built.

## Where I am now

I am a new student — still finding my rhythm, still collecting firsts. That is an honest place to be, and I like it. I want to learn, to meet people who care about things, and to build a life that is generous: good work, good friends, and a little more warmth in the rooms I enter than when I arrived.

I do not have the whole future mapped. I have energy, sympathy, and an open door. If you care to know me, I would be glad to know you too.

## Words I try to live by

- Be the person who waves first.
- Listen as if the other person might change your mind.
- Keep a light heart, and a serious kindness.
- Leave people feeling a little more seen than before.
